Please revise the implementation plan into a cost-conscious Phase 1 focused on structure, usability, and reusable components.

Do not attempt to fully build every future page, feature, animation, backend workflow, or content system in the first pass.

## Phase 1 Goal

Create the strong visual and structural foundation of the new website while preserving the best graphics, branding, colors, imagery, and personality from the current site.

The priority is to establish:

* Clear positioning
* Strong homepage structure
* Clean navigation
* Reusable service and project components
* Mobile responsiveness
* Consistent visual styling
* Easy future expansion

## Phase 1 Pages

Fully build only these core pages:

1. Home
2. Services
3. Projects / Experience
4. About
5. Contact

Create simple placeholder routes or navigation-ready placeholders for future pages, but do not fully develop them yet.

Future pages include:

* Markets We Serve
* Case Studies
* Financing & Tax Incentives
* Utility & Rebate Resources
* FAQ
* Manufacturer Pages
* Capabilities Statement
* Quote Request Wizard
* AI Assistant

## Homepage Scope

Build the homepage with these sections:

* Hero
* Who We Work With
* Core Practice Areas
* Why Positive Energy
* Featured Projects / Experience
* Resilient Power and Remote Deployments
* Trust Builders
* Manufacturer and Organization Experience
* Geographic Service Area
* Final Call to Action

Keep the first version concise. Use temporary text or placeholders where final copy, customer approvals, photos, logos, or project details are not yet available.

## Services Scope

Create one Services overview page organized into reusable practice-area sections:

* Commercial Infrastructure
* Distributed Energy
* Field Services
* Engineering & Consulting
* Resilient Power

Do not build ten separate service landing pages during Phase 1.

Instead, structure the service data and components so individual landing pages can be added later without redesigning the site.

## Projects and Experience Scope

Create a reusable project-card system and a clean Projects / Experience page.

Initially populate it with a small number of representative entries and placeholders.

The component should support future fields such as:

* Project name
* Location
* Client type
* Industry
* Services performed
* Equipment
* Completion date
* Short summary
* Multiple photos
* Optional customer logo
* Tags

Do not build a complex database, CMS, advanced filtering system, or interactive map during Phase 1.

Use a simple editable local data file, JSON file, or similarly straightforward content structure that can later be upgraded.

## Design Guardrails

Preserve the strongest existing custom graphics and visual identity.

Do not:

* Replace custom graphics with generic stock images
* Redesign the logo
* Introduce a completely new color system
* Add excessive animations
* Add video backgrounds
* Add complex scroll effects
* Add decorative features that do not improve usability
* Rewrite every piece of existing content automatically
* Remove existing assets unless they are clearly broken, redundant, or low quality

Prefer:

* Clean spacing
* Strong typography
* Consistent buttons
* Reusable cards
* Clear section hierarchy
* Subtle transitions
* Fast loading
* Mobile-first layouts
* Accessible contrast and navigation

Before removing or significantly altering a major existing graphic, create a replacement proposal or retain the original until we review it.

## Technical and Expense Guardrails

Keep Phase 1 technically simple and inexpensive.

Do not add unless specifically approved:

* Paid APIs
* Paid plugins
* New monthly software subscriptions
* External CMS platforms
* Authentication
* Customer accounts
* Databases
* AI integrations
* Automated quote calculations
* Payment processing
* Complex backend services
* Advanced analytics platforms
* Third-party form services with recurring fees

Use Cloudflare-compatible, lightweight architecture where practical.

Prefer static or mostly static pages, reusable components, local content files, and simple forms.

For the Contact page, build the form layout and validation, but clearly identify whether an external email or form-handling service will be required before connecting one.

Do not create any service that could generate recurring costs without first explaining:

1. What it does
2. Why it is needed
3. The likely cost
4. The free or lower-cost alternative

## Work Process

Please work in checkpoints rather than trying to finish and polish the entire site in one large run.

### Checkpoint 1

* Navigation
* Homepage skeleton
* Global styles
* Reusable components
* Mobile layout

Stop for review before major polish.

### Checkpoint 2

* Services page
* Projects / Experience page
* About page
* Contact page

Stop for review again.

### Checkpoint 3

* Copy refinement
* Spacing
* Image placement
* Responsive cleanup
* Accessibility
* Final Phase 1 polish

Avoid repeated redesign passes unless we specifically request them.

## Deliverable

The Phase 1 result should feel complete enough to preview and evaluate, but it does not need to include every future feature.

The goal is to get the bones right first:

* Brand
* Structure
* Navigation
* Messaging hierarchy
* Core pages
* Reusable components
* Future-ready architecture

Please revise your proposed plan around this reduced Phase 1 scope before beginning the build. Clearly identify anything you believe still creates significant agent usage, technical complexity, or recurring expense.
