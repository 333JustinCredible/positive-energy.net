We are redesigning the Positive Energy website. This is not a complete rebranding or a blank-sheet redesign.

The current website already contains a great deal of thought, strong visual branding, quality graphics, and content that reflects who we are. I want to preserve the overall look, feel, colors, imagery, and personality wherever possible.

Instead, think of this as Version 2 of the website.

Your goal is to elevate the existing site into one that could confidently represent a company competing for large commercial, municipal, industrial, and energy infrastructure projects.

Please retain and improve the strongest parts of the current design while modernizing the layout, information architecture, messaging, navigation, responsiveness, and scalability.

Do not replace custom graphics, illustrations, or visual elements simply for the sake of change. If an existing design element is working well, improve around it rather than discarding it.

Design Philosophy

The website should feel like a premium engineering and construction company.

Keep what already works. Improve what doesn't.

Prioritize:

Clear messaging
Better organization
Less text and more visual hierarchy
Strong typography
Real project photography
Clean spacing
Mobile responsiveness
Fast performance
Accessibility

Avoid redesigning simply for the sake of redesign. Respect the visual identity we've already established while refining it into something that feels timeless, polished, and capable of representing a company pursuing significantly larger and more complex commercial projects.

The overarching goal is evolution, not replacement. Imagine that Positive Energy has grown substantially over the past several years, and the website needs to mature alongside it. Keep the recognizable brand we've built, but elevate the user experience, messaging, and structure to match the level of clients and projects we want to attract over the next decade.
